# Flat elemental spell damage

Seven new prefixes add Fire, Ice, Wind, Earth, Lightning, Light or Dark spell damage. They use the existing Spell Damage gear pool, eventual offensive-spell capability filter and Class-bounded flat elemental attack ranges. Flat weapon effectiveness is applied to rolled bounds once, as with other flat affixes. They occupy the usual damage prefix slots. Different added spell elements coexist; duplicate copies cannot occupy multiple slots on the same item. The wearer sums equipped spell bonuses, while weapon attack additions stay local to their originating hand.

Each existing offensive spell hit gains its own added components, scaled by that hit’s actual action, repeat and target multipliers. Frizz gains one combined hit; a spell with several actual damage hits gains the corresponding number. There is no division by hit count and no new attack or resource event. The same native critical decision and already drawn critical multiplier apply to each added component. Original and added elements retain their independent resistance, weakness or immunity. Spell Damage and applicable elemental/family bonuses keep the existing additive bucket and caps. Native mitigation, guards, metal exceptions, damage limits and final rounding remain authoritative. Healing, buffs, status and MP-only rows gain no damage.

The seven matching prefix shards are appended after the original 84 shard IDs. They participate in eligibility groups, empty-slot crafting, tier upgrades and the existing independent salvage rolls. All original affix values, identities, shard balances and artwork remain unchanged. No save file is modified by this update.
